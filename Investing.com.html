Investing.com - Nasdaq 100 futures pointed lower on Tuesday, weighed down by Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller’s comments supporting a Federal Reserve interest rate hike in December, while investors awaited a key U.S. consumer inflation report that could provide some clues into the central bank’s monetary policy outlook.

By 05:51 ET (10:51 GMT), the Nasdaq 100 futures contract had dropped by 103 points, or 0.4%, while S&P 500 futures were mostly unchanged and Dow futures had inched upby 151 points, or 0.3%.

Monday brought a strong rebound, with the Nasdaq Composite up 2.7% and the S&P 500 posting its best day since April 8, after a week of selling driven by doubts over AI spending and by Democratic midterm wins in states with big data-center plans. Chipmakers led, with the Philadelphia semiconductor index near 7% higher and AMD (NASDAQ:AMD) up over 13%, while Nvidia (NASDAQ:NVDA) fell 4.2% amid the probe involving its CEO.

Waller backs rate hike

The October CPI and Friday's PPI are now the focus, with a December quarter-point hike nearly fully priced according to CME FedWatch. Waller said in London he would support a hike even on soft data, and possibly a half-point move on hot data.

Lower oil prices, after Trump signalled further strikes on Iran were unlikely, eased inflation worries, and earnings from CoreWeave, Cisco and Applied Materials will serve as a read on AI investment.

Separately, Reuters reported that Anthropic may begin marketing its IPO as soon as Tuesday, after an initial filing showing about 867 million shares at $70 to $80 each was withdrawn as inaccurate pending a revised version.

(Ayushman Ojha contributed reporting)
