Wall Street panic sell-off sends VIX up almost 70%. Here’s what usually happens next

A Democratic sweep sparked an AI trade unwind and one of the biggest VIX spikes on record. Here’s why stocks tanked and what history says.

Lead Writer

KEY POINTS

  • A Democratic midterm sweep stoked fears of tighter oversight of AI and data centres, with voters backing data centre limits in 40 of 41 local ballot measures.
  • Chip selling spread to all 11 S&P 500 sectors as hedge funds banked gains and retail was on track for its heaviest net selling since March 2020.
  • The VIX jumped nearly 70%, a move that has usually preceded a stronger S&P 500 a month on.

Wall Street just went from a euphoric post-election rally, to its worst session since the April 2025 crash the next day.

Stocks climbed on Wednesday after the midterm result, then the S&P 500, Nasdaq and Dow dropped 4.3%, 5.5% and 3.4% a day later.

What drove the slide:

  • Political risk to AI. Voters backed data centre curbs in 40 of 41 ballot measures.
  • Broad contagion. Chip selling hit all 11 S&P 500 sectors.
  • Soft guidance. Qualcomm and Arm fell on weak outlooks.
  • Profit-taking. Nomura’s McElligott blamed hedge funds locking in year-end gains.
  • Forced selling. Vanda flagged systematic deleveraging and the heaviest retail net selling since March 2020.

Goldman Sachs said hedge funds resold hyperscaler shares bought on the rebound, and with gross exposure in the 97th percentile, crowded tech trades face more forced selling.

One of the largest VIX spikes on record

The VIX jumped almost 70% to 28.1, its third-largest rise since 1990; no pandemic-era session made the top ten.

After nine of the past 10 such 50%-plus spikes, the S&P 500 was higher a month later.

The result holds at 40%, 50% or 60% thresholds, with one-month gains two to four times normal.

Closing thoughts

Tougher scrutiny of data centres could slow Big Tech spending and ripple through the economy. Large one-day sell-offs have historically rewarded patient buyers, though a bigger downturn remains possible.
