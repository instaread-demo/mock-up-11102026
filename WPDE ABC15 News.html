FLORENCE, S.C. (WPDE) — Thousands of people filled downtown Florence Saturday for the South Carolina Pecan Music and Food Festival, an annual event featuring food, live music, vendors and a classic car show.

The festival is expected to generate nearly $3 million in economic impact for the area.

The local festival began more than two decades ago and has grown into what organizers call the world's largest pecan festival.

This year's event featured 280 vendors, including 70 food vendors, as well as a classic car show with more than 130 vehicles.

Collectors showed off their rides and swapped tips; Moses Walls came from Cheraw in his 1952 Ford Mainline and enjoyed seeing old friends.

A 1971 Chevrolet Impala joined the lineup.
