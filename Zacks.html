Is a Continued Rebound in SpaceX Stock Imminent After Musk's Lofty Revenue Forecast?

Key Takeaways

  • Elon Musk's $500 billion annualized revenue forecast for FY27 has fueled a rebound in SpaceX stock.
  • SpaceX's FY27 EPS estimates have surged 132% over the last 30 days, signaling confidence in its outlook.

Shares of Space Exploration Technologies (SPCX - Free Report) ) are rebounding after a broader tech selloff, fueled by CEO Elon Musk's exceptionally bullish revenue outlook.

After surging nearly 9% last Friday, SPCX has gained another 5% in Monday's trading session, although shares remain well below their 52-week high of $225.

How far the recovery runs may depend on the AI infrastructure and satellite businesses.

Musk's Ambitious $500 Billion Revenue Forecast

At Friday's Baron Investment Conference, Musk set personal targets of a $100 billion-plus revenue run rate by December 2026 and $500 billion a year later, even mentioning $1 trillion, while making clear this was not company guidance. AI computing, which drove a 161% jump in third-quarter revenue to $14.6 billion, is the main engine, with Starlink Mobile and a multiyear 5G infrastructure deal with Qualcomm (QCOM - Free Report) as a possible second one.

SpaceX's Earnings Outlook & Positive EPS Revisions

Analysts have lifted the fiscal 2027 EPS estimate 132% in a month, from $1.77 to $4.10, and roughly doubled the FY26 figure to $0.19, which would imply growth of about 2,000%. The main worry is capital spending, projected at $35-$40 billion for the fourth quarter.

SPCX's Valuation Could Become Much More Attractive

The stock looks expensive at about 52 times trailing sales, but at a roughly $1.9 trillion market value it would trade near 3.8 times sales if the $500 billion run rate arrives, a little below its industry and the S&P 500. That figure rests on projected, not actual, revenue.

Bottom Line: Is The Rebound in SPCX Just Beginning?

With a Zacks Rank #3 (Hold), the article suggests waiting for now, though continued estimate increases could earn a buy rating and extend the rebound.
